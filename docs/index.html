2. Apple HealthKit

♡ HealthKit Data Protection

If you grant permission, Symptly reads sleep, heart rate, step count, and active minutes from Apple Health. This data is used exclusively on your device to generate health correlations and insights. We never upload, transmit, sell, or share HealthKit data with any third party, advertising network, or data broker. HealthKit data is never used for marketing or advertising purposes.

You can revoke HealthKit access at any time in Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Symptly.

3. iCloud Sync

Your health data syncs across your Apple devices through your personal iCloud account using Apple's CloudKit framework. This means:

  • Data is encrypted in transit and at rest by Apple
  • Only devices signed into your Apple ID can access your data
  • We cannot access, read, or decrypt your iCloud data
  • You can disable iCloud sync in your device's iCloud settings

4. Data We Do NOT Collect

  • We do not collect your name, email, or any account credentials
  • We do not use analytics, tracking pixels, or third-party SDKs
  • We do not serve advertisements
  • We do not sell, rent, or share any data with third parties
  • We do not use your data for machine learning model training
  • We have no servers that receive your health information

5. On-Device Processing

All analysis, correlations, wellness scoring, and predictions run entirely on your device using Apple's CoreML framework. No health data is ever sent to external servers or cloud APIs for processing.

6. Data Storage & Security

  • Local data is stored using Apple's SwiftData framework with device-level encryption
  • Data is protected by your device passcode, Face ID, or Touch ID
  • iCloud data is encrypted using Apple's security infrastructure
  • We use no custom encryption; we rely on Apple's proven, audited security stack

7. Data Retention & Deletion

Your data exists only as long as you keep it. You can:

  • Export all data as CSV from Settings > Data > Export Data
  • Delete individual entries by swiping left in History
  • Clear all data from Settings > Data > Clear All Data
  • Reset the app from Settings > Account > Reset App
  • Delete the app to remove all local data permanently

When you delete data, it is removed from your device and your iCloud storage. Due to iCloud propagation, deletion may take up to 7 days to sync across all devices.

8. Children's Privacy

Symptly is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has used the app, the data exists only on that device and can be deleted by removing the app.

9. Third-Party Services

Symptly uses the following Apple system services only:

  • Apple HealthKit: read health data (with your permission)
  • Apple CloudKit: sync data via your iCloud account
  • Apple StoreKit: process subscription purchases
  • Apple WeatherKit / Open-Meteo: fetch weather data for correlations

No other third-party services, SDKs, or analytics tools are used.

10. Changes to This Policy

Getting Started

How do I log a symptom?

Tap the + button on the home screen. Select your symptom, rate its severity (1-10), add optional triggers and notes, then save. Your entry appears in History immediately.

What's the Wellness Score?

Your Wellness Score (0-100) is calculated from your onboarding answers and daily tracking data. It reflects your overall symptom burden, lifestyle factors, and trends. Higher is better. The score updates as you log more data.

How does the daily check-in work?

Each day, the home screen prompts you to rate your mood and energy level. You can also log sleep hours, stress level, and water intake. These feed into your lifestyle correlations.

Insights & Correlations

How are correlations calculated?

Symptly uses statistical methods (Pearson correlation, Welch's t-test) to find patterns in your data. You need at least 5 data points over 30 days for meaningful correlations. More data = better insights.

What is a flare-up alert?

When your recent 3-day symptom severity exceeds your 14-day baseline by 1.5+ points, or your logging frequency doubles, Symptly flags a potential flare-up with severity tiers (mild, moderate, severe).

Data & Privacy

How do I export my data?

Go to Settings > Data > Export Data. Your symptom entries, check-ins, and medications are exported as a CSV file that you can share with your healthcare provider.

Does my data sync across devices?

Yes. If you're signed into iCloud, your data automatically syncs across all your Apple devices. This uses Apple's CloudKit, so we never see your data.

How do I delete all my data?

Settings > Data > Clear All Data removes all symptom entries, journals, and medications. Settings > Account > Reset App also clears your onboarding profile. Deleting the app removes everything from the device.

Subscriptions

How do I cancel my subscription?

Open the Settings app on your iPhone > Tap your name > Subscriptions > Symptly > Cancel Subscription. You'll keep access until the end of your billing period.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

Sign into the same Apple ID. Symptly automatically detects your active subscription via StoreKit. If it doesn't appear, go to the paywall screen and tap "Restore Purchases."

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are handled by Apple. Visit reportaproblem.apple.com to request one.

Troubleshooting

HealthKit data isn't showing up

Go to Settings > Privacy & Security > Health > Symptly and make sure all categories are enabled. Then reopen Symptly; it fetches the latest data on launch.

My data disappeared after updating

Your data migrates automatically from the old storage format. If something went wrong, your iCloud backup may have your data. Contact us and we'll help troubleshoot.

Your Data Rights

You have complete control over your health data.

Right to Access

All your data is visible directly in the app. Use Settings > Data > Export Data to download a complete CSV of your symptom history, check-ins, and medications.

Right to Deletion

You can delete individual entries, clear all data, or reset the entire app. We don't retain backups of your data. Once deleted, it's gone.

🔒 Data deletion is permanent

When you clear your data or reset the app, it's removed from your device and iCloud. There is no recovery option. Export your data first if you want a backup.

Right to Portability

Your data can be exported as CSV at any time. This standard format is compatible with spreadsheet apps, healthcare portals, and other health tracking tools.

Right to Restrict Processing

You can disable individual features:

  • HealthKit: toggle off in Settings > Tracking > Apple Health
  • Notifications: toggle off in Settings > Tracking > Reminders
  • iCloud sync: disable in your device's iCloud settings
