**Who:** Keshav Krishnan — solo founder, builder, health tech
**What:** Building Symptly, a privacy-first health tracking app with on-device ML

## Phase Timeline

### Phase 1: The Builder (Weeks 1–2)

**Theme:** "I'm building an app with AI. Here's what that actually looks like."

**Content focus (80% of posts):**

- Claude Code, Cursor, or whatever AI tools are in your stack
- Before/after screenshots of UI as you iterate
- The honest mess of solo development
- Decisions you made and why (design, architecture, feature cuts)
- Mistakes, dead ends, things you'd do differently

**Supporting content (20%):**
- React to AI/tech news with your builder perspective
- Engage with other founders building with AI

**Goal:** People know you as "that person building a health app with AI tools." No product marketing yet. Just the craft.

**Milestones to hit before moving to Phase 2:**
- [ ] 50+ followers
- [ ] 5+ meaningful conversations with other builders
- [ ] 3+ posts that got >10 likes

## Voice Guardrails

### Always
- First person ("I built", "I noticed", "I struggled")
- Specific details over vague claims ("3 hours debugging" not "a long time")
- Acknowledge uncertainty ("I think", "not sure yet", "still figuring out")
- Reference real moments, real times, real emotions

### Never
- Third person about yourself ("The founder of Symptly...")
- Marketing speak ("revolutionary", "game-changing", "disruptive")
- Guru tone ("Here's the secret to...", "5 steps to...")
- Fake urgency ("You NEED to see this", "Don't miss out")
- Humble bragging ("I accidentally got 10k downloads")
