> Daily posting, replying, and engagement targets for Claude Cowork + Claude in Chrome automation.
> Warm-up strategy to avoid shadowbans. Gradual ramp from human-only to full AI-assisted.

## Account Warm-Up Phases

### Phase 1: Foundation (Days 1-14): MANUAL ONLY

No automation. Everything is you. The algorithm needs to learn you're real.

| Action | Daily Target | Tool |
|--------|-------------|------|
| Original posts | 1-2 | Manual (phone/browser) |
| Replies to others | 5-8 | Manual |
| Likes | 10-15 | Manual |
| Quote tweets | 0-1 | Manual |
| DMs to founders | 1-2 | Manual |
| Browse feed | 15-20 min | Manual |

**Rules:**
- Post at varied times (not the same minute every day)
- Mix text-only, image, and reply posts
- Engage with accounts 10x–100x your size
- Complete your profile: real photo, bio, pinned tweet, banner
- Get X Premium immediately ($8/mo for trust signal + reach)

## Daily Automation Workflow

### Morning Routine (Automated: 7:00 AM)
1. Cowork checks `BRAND_EVOLUTION.md` for current phase content themes
2. Cowork drafts 3 posts for the day based on current themes
3. Posts saved to `POST_CATALOG.md` with status `scheduled`
4. Chrome posts first tweet of the day at 8:00 AM

### Midday (Automated: 12:00 PM)
1. Chrome posts second tweet at 12:30 PM
2. Cowork scans target accounts for new posts
3. Cowork drafts 10 reply suggestions → `COMMENTS_PLAYBOOK.md`
4. Notification sent to you for review

### Your Manual Block (15-30 min: whenever fits)
1. Review drafted replies in `COMMENTS_PLAYBOOK.md` → approve/edit/reject
2. Chrome posts approved replies
3. Respond to anyone who engaged with your posts
4. Send 2-3 DMs to new connections
5. Post 1 spontaneous tweet (something real from your day)

### Evening (Automated: 5:00 PM)
1. Chrome posts third tweet at 5:30 PM
2. Cowork logs all day's activity to `POST_CATALOG.md`
3. Cowork checks `TECH_INNOVATIONS.md` for trending topics → drafts tomorrow's content

### Night (Automated: 9:00 PM, Phase 4+)
1. Chrome posts evening engagement tweet (poll, question, or raw thought)
2. Cowork compiles daily metrics → appends to `POST_CATALOG.md` weekly summary

## Posting Time Slots (EST)

| Slot | Time | Content Type | Why |
|------|------|-------------|-----|
| Early bird | 7:30-8:30 AM | Insight, lesson, or motivation | Catches morning scrollers |
| Midday | 12:00-1:00 PM | Build update, screenshot, tool tip | Lunch break engagement |
| Afternoon | 3:00-4:00 PM | Thread or deep take | Post-meeting decompression |
| Evening | 5:30-6:30 PM | Question, poll, or community post | End-of-day browsing |
| Night | 9:00-10:00 PM | Raw thought, honest moment | Late-night personal connection |

**Vary these by ±30 minutes daily.** Bots post at exact timestamps. Humans don't.

## Shadowban Prevention Rules

1. **Never jump more than 2x volume day-over-day.** If you posted 4 yesterday, don't post 10 today.
2. **No identical or near-identical text** across any two posts within 7 days.
3. **Space replies 2-5 minutes apart.** Rapid-fire replies = bot signal.
4. **Don't follow/unfollow.** This is the #1 trigger. Follow people you genuinely want to follow. Never unfollow to manage ratios.
5. **Mix content types.** Text, images, polls, threads, quote tweets, never just one type.
6. **Keep some posts with zero links.** Link-heavy accounts get suppressed.
7. **If impressions drop 80%+ overnight:** stop all automation for 48 hours, delete any promotional/repetitive content, increase manual browsing/liking, then resume at 50% volume.

## Weekly Review Checklist

Every Sunday, Cowork should run this audit:

- [ ] Review impressions trend: growing, flat, or declining?
- [ ] Check top 3 performing posts: what format? What topic?
- [ ] Check worst 3 posts: what flopped? Why?
- [ ] Update `BRAND_EVOLUTION.md` phase if milestones hit
- [ ] Refresh target account list (add new, remove inactive)
- [ ] Replenish `TECH_INNOVATIONS.md` with fresh topics
- [ ] Archive old posts in `POST_CATALOG.md`
- [ ] Adjust daily volume if needed based on engagement rates
