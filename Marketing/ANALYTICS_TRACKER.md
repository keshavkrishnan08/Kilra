## How to Use This File

**Cowork (every Sunday):**
1. Pull metrics from X Analytics (or ask Keshav to share a screenshot)
2. Fill in the current week's row in the Growth table
3. Fill in the Content Performance section
4. Write 2-3 sentences of analysis — what worked, what didn't, what to change

## Red Flags — When to Change Strategy

| Signal | What It Means | What to Do |
|--------|--------------|-----------|
| Impressions drop 50%+ week-over-week | Possible shadowban or content miss | Pause automation 48h, check for spam signals, increase manual engagement |
| Engagement rate below 2% for 2+ weeks | Content isn't resonating | Test different formats, go back to what worked before, ask audience what they want |
| Follower growth stalls for 2+ weeks | Reached ceiling of current audience | Expand to new audience segment, increase reply volume to bigger accounts |
| Getting followers but no replies | Attracting passive followers, not community | Post more questions and polls, engage more in others' conversations |
| High impressions but low follows | Content is interesting but profile isn't converting | Update bio, pinned tweet, and profile photo |
